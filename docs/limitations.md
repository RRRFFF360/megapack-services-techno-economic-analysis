# Limitations

- Perfect foresight: historical prices are known for each optimization day. The result is a hindsight-optimal benchmark under the specified constraints, not attainable forecast revenue or a guarantee.
- DA energy arbitrage only; no Real-Time trading, ancillary services, Resource Adequacy, PPA/tolling or other contracted revenues. These revenue streams cannot simply be added without considering mutually competing capacity and dispatch commitments.
- NP15 aggregate hub prices are not site-specific nodal settlements. No market impact, bid/offer friction, market participation constraints, trading fees or imbalance costs.
- No taxes, incentives, financing, site-specific interconnection, balance-of-plant costs or complete project CAPEX.
- No full degradation, calendar aging, augmentation, thermal derating, auxiliary loads, major replacement or end-of-life model. No additional SOC operating reserve/window. The modeled power/energy convention may differ from the product's AC usable-capacity convention.
- Daily fixed 50% SOC boundaries exclude interday optimization. EFC constraints use internal discharged energy; different conventions change results.
- Repeating one historical year for 15 years does not forecast future volatility, market saturation or regulation.
- Maintenance and hardware figures are user-supplied historical estimates, not independently reverified current quotations. Currency price years are not reconciled.
- Availability is approximated proportionally. Actual lost value depends on outage timing, market conditions, contractual exposure and restoration performance.
- Service recommendations are product hypotheses. This study has no customer interviews, validated willingness-to-pay estimate or evidence of Tesla's internal roadmap.

“Value floor” refers only to the intentionally narrow **revenue scope**. It is not a mathematical lower bound on realized project revenue: perfect foresight and omitted costs can overstate DA value. Prefer “DA-only value baseline.” The operating PV is not full project valuation and does not establish profitability.

Independent educational analysis; not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla or CAISO.

The customer segmentation map is qualitative and illustrative, not an output of dispatch optimization or measured customer research. Delivery paths overlap. High revenue exposure does not establish a preference for Tesla-managed service. No provider bids, customer interviews, service-specific failure rates or willingness-to-pay estimates validate the hypotheses.
